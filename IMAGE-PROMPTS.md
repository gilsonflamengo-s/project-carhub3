# Image generation

Mode: built-in image_gen (no API-key CLI). Generated PNG originals remain in the Codex generated_images directory. The project ships optimised WebP derivatives at the paths below.

## dist/assets/hero.webp

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a photorealistic cinematic automotive editorial photograph, landscape 1536x1024. A pristine deep graphite grey sports coupe seen front three-quarter, nose facing left, photographed low, centered in the lower two thirds, entire car visible. Dark premium detailing studio, black walls, a few clean white linear overhead light reflections along glossy bodywork, slightly wet dark concrete floor, moody charcoal atmosphere with subtle olive undertone. Natural photographic realism, meticulous black multi-spoke wheels, crisp headlight details, no obvious brand badges, no people, no letters, no text, no watermarks. Broad dark negative space in top third for website headline overlay. Luxury editorial ad quality, restrained lighting, no neon sci-fi effects. This is an illustrative concept asset, not a picture of an actual business.

## dist/assets/detail.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Landscape editorial photograph 1536x1024 for a luxury automotive detailing website. Extreme close-up of a professional's black nitrile-gloved hands carefully using a small round dual-action polishing machine on the curved graphite-black hood of a luxury car. Rich glossy paint with clean white strip-light reflections, moody dark detailing bay in the background, tangible surface textures, shallow depth of field, photographic realistic hands and tool, restrained black and neutral palette. Composition: paint surface sweeps diagonally, tool and hands in right half with elegant reflected lines extending left. No faces, no text, no logos, no watermark. An illustrative studio concept, not documentary evidence of a real company.

## dist/assets/interior.webp

Full-frame DSLR magazine photography. Illustrative concept for detailing website.
