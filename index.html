Which service is right for my car?

It depends on your car’s condition and your priorities. A valet focuses on cleaning, while paint correction and coating address different aspects of the finish. Tell the team what you want to improve and ask for a recommendation.

How much does a detail cost?

Request a personalised quote. The vehicle’s size, condition and chosen service affect the work involved. Confirm the scope and price directly before booking.

How long will you need my car?

Timing depends on the service and condition of your vehicle. Ask the team for an estimated completion time when discussing your quote.

What is the difference between correction and coating?

Paint correction refines the existing finish to improve the appearance of certain surface imperfections. A ceramic coating adds a protective layer. The team can advise on suitable preparation and care for your car.

Does a quote request confirm my booking?

No. Contact Carhub directly to confirm your quote, appointment and any preparation needed before your visit.
